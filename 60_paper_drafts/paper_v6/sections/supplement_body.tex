\section*{Supplementary material: the case study in full}
\noindent This supplement holds the full report of the descriptive case study on Indian rooftop-solar regulation that
the paper summarises in its final appendix: the reference data and their human audit, the conditions, damage and
scoring, the planned analyses and their results, the readings fixed in advance, the prompts, the per-clause results,
the annotation agreement, the deviations from the analysis plan and the data statement. The case study's answer key
failed the quality checks planned for it, so nothing here supports a finding of the main paper; it is reported for
transparency, with its planned analyses and readings kept as they were fixed. The sections are carried over from the
study's own report, written before the RFC study, and use its terms (reviewer for judge, registry for record, clause
for document).
